# Drive Soundtrack

Build a complete, production-ready multi-page website for Veeren Drive Connect at veerendriveconnect.com. Establish the brand system first, including an original custom vector logo system: a premium minimal geometric abstract symbol evoking automotive motion, road curve, sound/connectivity and negative space, not literal vehicle/headphone/radio imagery and not resembling SiriusXM; use it as primary horizontal wordmark VEEREN DRIVE CONNECT, compact symbol plus VEEREN, symbol-only favicon, and light/dark variants. Integrate it across header, mobile nav, footer, legal/contact pages, metadata and an almost-instant tasteful loading motion.

Create a premium cinematic automotive technology + connected audio editorial experience, visibly original and not a generic SaaS, dealership, cable site, or SiriusXM imitation. Palette: deep charcoal/graphite/near-black/off-white/soft metallic gray with tightly controlled automotive red accents. Typography: strong modern sans editorial display with readable body, thin technical borders, sharp containers, logo-derived motion lines/waveforms/road curves/nodes. Use high-quality realistic modern automotive interiors, dashboards, infotainment, cabins, night driving and relevant driving imagery, with responsive optimized use, meaningful alt text, object positioning, lazy loading. Avoid fake stats, testimonials, awards, official/authorized/dealer claims, guarantees, fake policies, addresses, contacts and business hours. Position as transparent guidance, information, assistance, support and exploration, including SiriusXM-related information/assistance but never presenting Veeren as SiriusXM or an official representative.

Build these complete pages with a unique title/meta description, canonical and OG data, unique H1 and sound semantic H2 hierarchy each: Home, About, Services, Audio Experience, Vehicle Audio, FAQ, Contact/Assistance, Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, Refund Policy, Disclaimer. Footer must link to all.

Homepage: cinematic full-bleed hero (CONNECTED AUDIO EXPERIENCE, 'Your Drive. Your Soundtrack.', role as connected in-car audio information/assistance, CTAs Explore Audio Services and Get Assistance), technical metadata, scroll cue, image/light movement and animated entrance. Editorial asymmetric 'The Drive Has Changed' evolution section with image/data details; signature Interactive Vehicle Audio Experience using dashboard visual and clickable/keyboard accessible hotspots for satellite radio, music, talk, news, sports, entertainment, podcasts and connectivity, contextual details; horizontal editorial Audio Categories gallery (Music, News, Sports, Talk, Entertainment, Podcasts, In-Car Audio) with category numbers; full bleed 'Built Around Your Drive' vehicle image with floating commute/road trip/weekend/family/late-night points; interactive four-stage assistance timeline (tell needs, understand vehicle, explore options, get guidance) horizontal desktop and vertical mobile; informational Vehicle Compatibility interface with selections for vehicle type/model year/audio system/connectivity and a clear no-real-database informational result, never fake lookup; asymmetric Why Veeren editorial section; magazine-style featured insights with useful actual subjects; finish with distinct cinematic night-driving CTA 'Make Every Mile Sound Better.'

About: entirely distinct editorial hero, story, mission, what it does, customer assistance, core principles, transparent positioning, visual timeline, automotive imagery, CTA.
Services: applicable services only: In-Car Audio Guidance, SiriusXM Information & Assistance, Vehicle Audio Support, Connected Entertainment Guidance, Service Information, General Customer Assistance. Image-led large panels, numbered nav, hover previews/expandable details, not a generic card grid.
Audio Experience: immersive music/sports/news/talk/entertainment/podcasts/long-drive listening layouts, horizontal storytelling and 'Choose Your Drive' interactive selector (Commute, Road Trip, Relax, Sports, News, Music) that changes content/visual treatment.
Vehicle Audio: automotive interiors/dashboards/infotainment/audio system/connectivity/compatibility guidance and transparent FAQs with technical UI styling.
FAQ: categorized accessible animated accordions: General, Vehicle & Compatibility, Audio, SiriusXM-related Questions, Assistance, Billing / Service Questions, all factual and transparent.
Contact/Assistance: split automotive visual/form with Name, Email, Phone, Vehicle Information, Reason for Contact, Message. Fully validated with focus states and explicit loading/success/error states (front-end demo acceptable but no false submission claim), response-expectations and privacy/disclaimer language, business contact/hours only as clearly marked placeholders where not supplied.
Legal pages: polished reading layouts, clear structured placeholders for business-specific details/policy info that was not provided; no invented claims.

Add a premium sticky desktop header with all named navigation routes and Get Assistance CTA, compacting on scroll; mobile compact logo and animated accessible menu. Use refined animations: page transitions, scroll reveals/stagger, restrained parallax/image scales, technical lines, hover states, button microinteractions, scroll progress, smooth anchors; respect prefers-reduced-motion; no gaudy effects. Responsive behavior must be specifically designed for mobile, no overflow, touch-ready galleries/components. Implement semantic HTML, keyboard navigation including hotspots and selectors, visible focus, form validation, and performance-conscious images/motion. Use React/TypeScript/Tailwind and Motion if supported. Ensure it builds successfully. Prioritize depth and polish across routes rather than leaving sparse placeholder pages.
